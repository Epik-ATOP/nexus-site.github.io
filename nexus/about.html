ABOUT US

CIS-Nexus is a team filled with innovative professionals, hard working coaches, and curious students. Ever since 2022, we have strived to inspire public school students with the knowledge and skills required to become transformative members of the STEAM community. All members of the team have been people who have a passion for robotics and want to make an impact in the lives of children in our area through the teaching of STEAM. So far, we have made amazing progress.
